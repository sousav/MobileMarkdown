MobileMarkdown

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

MobileMarkdown is a lightweight mobile app for opening and reading Markdown files offline. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Short version: MobileMarkdown does not require an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not upload your Markdown files to our servers.

Data Collection

MobileMarkdown does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require account creation or sign-in.

Files and Content

Markdown files you open remain on your device. The app reads local files so it can display them to you, but it is designed for offline use and does not transmit those files to a remote server.

Local Storage

The app may store a small amount of information locally on your device, including:

  • your recent files list
  • theme preference such as light, dark, or system
  • other small settings required for the app to work as expected

This information stays on your device.

Network Use

MobileMarkdown is intended to work offline. The release app does not use analytics, advertising, or background data collection services.

External Links

If you tap a web link inside a Markdown document, the link opens in your device's browser or the system handler for that link. Any third-party site you visit is governed by its own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

MobileMarkdown is not designed to collect personal information from children or adults. It is a simple file viewer and does not provide account-based services.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please use the project repository or issue tracker:

  • github.com/sousav/MobileMarkdown
  • github.com/sousav/MobileMarkdown/issues

Package Name

This policy applies to the Android app package com.mobilemarkdown.mobile_markdown and the MobileMarkdown app published by this project.
